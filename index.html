Sync

Sync scanned business cards straight into Google Contacts or Office 365.

Connect your Google or Microsoft account once, then push your whole library into its contacts with a single click — no CSV files, no copy-paste.

  • One click, both ways of tidy — new cards are added, edits update, removed cards drop off
  • No duplicates — every contact is matched by a hidden ID, even after a reinstall or a new Mac
  • Every field mapped — name, company, title, emails, phones, Business Fax, and full postal address
  • Works from iPhone & Mac — the same one-click sync action on both
  • Two address books — Google Contacts and Office 365 / Outlook, each into your own account and nowhere else
  • Apple Contacts too — save any card straight into the address book already on your iPhone or Mac
  • Four file formats — vCard, CSV, Outlook-ready CSV and Salesforce-ready CSV
  • Or your own CRM — post each card you save to a web address you choose; off until you switch it on

What cardlio does not take from your Google account.

It reads back only the contacts it created itself, found by a marker it wrote. The rest of your Google Contacts are never requested and never received, the card photo is never sent, and sync runs one way — cardlio to Google — and only when you ask for it. Disconnect in Settings and the sign-in token is deleted; the contacts already in your account stay exactly as they are.

Ask

Ask your network a question.

“Who did I meet in Singapore who works in shipping?” Ask it the way you'd say it, and cardlio answers with the cards the answer came from — so you can check it rather than take its word. Your own device does the searching, so an invented colleague can't appear.

  • Ask Siri about the people you met

    “Text the person from Tristar” — Siri knows your cards as people, from the index already on your device.

  • Point the camera at a card

    Camera Control or visual intelligence, tap Search, and cardlio opens with the name and company already read.

  • Apple's Private Cloud Compute, no key needed

    Apple's larger model polishes names, titles and companies and reads the address from the photo — no account, and Apple keeps nothing.

Siri, the camera hand-off and Private Cloud Compute need iOS 27 or macOS 27 and Apple Intelligence.

Private by design

Your network is nobody's business.

cardlio was built the old-fashioned way: your data is yours. No accounts, no tracking, no servers reading your contacts.

  • On-device — scanning, reading, and organizing happen on your phone or Mac
  • Your private iCloud — sync uses Apple's cloud, encrypted for you alone
  • Links with nothing behind them — a shared card lives inside the link itself, never on a server
  • No signal, no problem — scanning is fully offline, so it works exactly the same in a conference basement as on your desk
  • Built to outlive us — your library lives on your devices and in your iCloud, in Apple's own contact formats; nothing about it depends on our servers existing
  • No analytics — not in the app, not on this website

iPhone + Mac + any browser

One library. Every machine.

Scan on your iPhone at the conference, tidy up on your Mac at your desk. Same cards, everywhere, instantly.

And on a Windows PC, an Android phone or a borrowed screen: sign in at team.cardlio.app with your Apple Account to search all your cards, with their photos. Rate a lead, add a note after a call or mark a follow-up done, and it is back on your iPhone and Mac a moment later. Downloading them as vCard, CSV or ZIP comes with the unlock. Your browser talks to your own iCloud — there is no server of ours in between.

Available worldwide on the App Store. Requires iOS 26.5 or macOS 26.5. Siri answers, the camera hand-off and Apple’s Private Cloud Compute need iOS 27 or macOS 27.
