A business card scanner for Mac

cardlio for Mac · November 2026

cardlio is a native Mac app that turns business cards into complete contacts, street address included. Drop in photos of cards or a PDF of scanned pages, check what it read in a sortable table, and send the contacts to Apple Contacts, Google Contacts, Office 365 or your CRM. The text is read on your Mac, there is no account to create, and it is one purchase, not a subscription.

Getting cards in

  • Drag and drop. Drag card photos or PDFs into the window, drop them on cardlio's Dock icon, or use Open With → cardlio in Finder. A PDF or a whole folder of scans is read in one go.
  • Scan with iPhone. Use your iPhone or iPad as the Mac's camera, straight from the toolbar.
  • Email signatures. Select a signature in Mail or Outlook and choose Services → Send Signature to cardlio, or drag the selected text into the window.
  • Coming from another card app? cardlio reads vCard, CSV and Excel exports and skips anyone you already have.

Checking a big pile

  • A table view with one row per card — photo, name, title, company, email, phone, city, country. Sort by any column, hide the ones you don't need; an empty field shows a dash.
  • Flags, not guesswork. Fields cardlio wasn't sure of are marked before saving, and every address is checked against Apple Maps.
  • The keyboard works. Arrow keys move through cards, Space shows a card big with Live Text, ⌘F searches.
  • Find Duplicates merges people you met twice into one contact, keeping every email and note.

Getting contacts out

Save to Apple Contacts, sync to Google Contacts or Office 365 with one click, export vCard, CSV, Outlook-ready or Salesforce-ready CSV, or post each new card to a CRM webhook. How cards reach HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive.

With your iPhone, or without it

The Mac app works on its own. If you also use cardlio on iPhone, scan at the conference and tidy up at your desk: the same library syncs through your own iCloud. On a Windows PC, sign in at team.cardlio.app with your Apple Account to search your cards in any browser.

Questions

Can I scan business cards on a Mac without an iPhone?

Yes. Drop card photos or a PDF of scanned cards into the window and cardlio reads them on the Mac. An iPhone or iPad can also act as the Mac's camera with Scan with iPhone, but it is not required.

Do I need to buy cardlio twice for iPhone and Mac?

The iPhone and Mac apps are sold separately, each with a one-time unlock and the first 10 cards free. Your library syncs between them either way, through your own iCloud.

Are my cards uploaded to read them?

No. The text is read on your Mac. There is no cardlio account and no cardlio server that stores your cards; sync uses your own iCloud.

Get cardlio

A free download on the Mac App Store, and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Your first 10 cards are free. Requires macOS 26.5. Questions: support@cardlio.app.
