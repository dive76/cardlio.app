Send your cards to any CRM

Recipes for Zapier and Make · cardlio 3.2 and later

cardlio can post every card you save to a web address you choose. Point that address at Zapier or Make and each new card can become a contact in HubSpot, a lead in Salesforce, a person in Pipedrive, a row in Google Sheets — or go to almost any of the thousands of apps those services connect to. No plug-in to install, nothing extra to buy from us, and cardlio needs no account.

The webhook itself costs nothing extra in cardlio. Zapier and Make have their own free and paid plans; a simple two-step Zap or Make scenario usually fits a free plan.

1. In cardlio

  1. Open Settings → CRM on the Mac, or Settings on iPhone and iPad, and find Send new cards to a webhook.
  2. Paste the address Zapier or Make gives you (below) and switch the toggle on.
  3. Tap Send test. cardlio posts a made-up contact — never one of yours — so you can map the fields before any real card goes out.

From then on every card you save is posted once, as it is saved. Switching the webhook on does not send your existing library. The card photo is never sent.

2a. With Zapier

  1. In Zapier, create a Zap. For the trigger choose Webhooks by Zapier and the event Catch Hook. Copy the URL it shows (it starts with https://hooks.zapier.com/hooks/catch/) into cardlio.
  2. Tap Send test in cardlio, then Test trigger in Zapier. It finds the test contact, “Wei Ming Tan, Meridian Shipping”, with every field listed.
  3. Add a Filter step: Only continue if Is Test → (Boolean) Is false. Your test contact then never reaches your CRM.
  4. Add the action for your system and map the fields — see the tables below. Turn the Zap on.

2b. With Make

  1. In Make, create a scenario. Add the Webhooks app, trigger Custom webhook, click Add, name it “cardlio”, and copy the address into cardlio.
  2. While Make shows “waiting for data”, tap Send test in cardlio. Make reports Successfully determined and now knows every field.
  3. Between the webhook and the next module, set a filter: isTest → Equal to → false.
  4. Add the module for your system and map the fields — see below. Switch scheduling to Immediately and turn the scenario on.

Two fields are lists: emails and leadInterests. In Make, use get(emails; 1) for the first address and join(leadInterests; ", ") for the interests. Zapier shows them as text you can map directly.

Google SheetsOne row per card

Make a sheet with a header row — for example Scanned, First name, Last name, Title, Company, Email, Phone, Mobile, City, Country, Event, Lead rating, Interests, Notes. Zapier: Google Sheets → Create Spreadsheet Row. Make: Google Sheets → Add a Row. Map one cardlio field to each column.

Anything else

Zoho CRM, Microsoft Dynamics, Airtable, Notion, monday.com, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Mailchimp — the steps are the same: the cardlio trigger above, then that app’s “create contact” or “create record” action. A Slack or Teams message (“New hot lead from SMM: Wei Ming Tan, Meridian Shipping”) is a nice way to tell the team at the stand.

4. Every field cardlio sends

One JSON object per card, sent as an HTTP POST with Content-Type: application/json. Every key is always present; a field the card does not have is an empty string (or an empty list).

Good to know

  • Sent once, when you save. Editing a card later, or changing its rating, does not post it again.
  • No automatic retries. A duplicate lead is more annoying than a missing one. If a post fails, cardlio shows a banner with Send again, so nothing is lost silently.
  • Avoid duplicates in your CRM with a “find or create” action matched on the email, or by storing cardlio’s id in a custom field.
  • https only. The address must begin with https://. Zapier and Make put a secret token in the address itself — treat the whole address like a password.
  • Where your cards go. This is the only cardlio feature that sends your cards to a destination you choose, and it is off until you switch it on. Once a card reaches your Zapier or Make address, it is handled by that service under its terms — not by cardlio. No server of ours is in the path. See the privacy policy.
