Business cards from shipping and maritime fairs

cardlio for iPhone, iPad and Mac · November 2026

Shipping is an international business, and so are its business cards: a chartering manager in Singapore, a shipyard in Japan, a ship manager in Hamburg, an owner in Oslo. cardlio reads all of them into complete contacts — Japanese, Chinese and Korean cards included — tags them with the fair where you met, and later finds the right person when you only remember the port and the trade.

Cards from every port

  • 28 languages, read on your device — Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Norwegian, Danish, Dutch, German, Turkish, Arabic and more. The full list.
  • Both sides, one contact. A Japanese front and an English back merge into the same person; the second-language name is kept as an alternate.
  • Translated on the spot. Chinese and Japanese cards are translated on your device; the translation stays with the card and is searchable.
  • Several offices on one card are kept apart, each address in its country's own format, checked on Apple Maps, with the local time there.
  • Titles kept as printed. An honorific such as Capt. shows when the card has one.
  • European companies: a VAT number on the card is checked in VIES, and the registered name can be copied in with one tap.

At SMM, Nor-Shipping or Marintec

  • Event mode tags every card you scan with the fair. Rate each person Hot, Warm or Cold before the next visitor reaches the stand.
  • One pile for the whole stand. A team library lets colleagues add cards to one shared pile and claim the leads they will follow up. Colleagues on Windows or Android join at team.cardlio.app.
  • No hall Wi-Fi needed. Scanning works offline; sync and the address check catch up later.
  • After the fair, one Event Report lists everyone you met, hot leads first, and Follow-Up Run drafts an e-mail for each. You send them from your own mail app. The trade-show guide.

Your own sectors

The built-in industries are broad. Add the ones your world uses — Chartering, Ship management, Shipbroking, Port agency, Bunkering, Classification — under Settings → Industries & Interests. Tagging, search filters and the Stats chart then use your list.

Find them again

  • Ask in plain words: “Who did I meet in Singapore who works in shipping?” cardlio answers with the cards the answer came from, so you can check it.
  • The map shows your contacts city by city, country by country.
  • Companies change names. On the Mac, Check Email Domains… lists the company domains that no longer exist — usually a merger, a rename or a closure — so you know whose card is out of date.

Questions

Can cardlio read Japanese, Chinese and Korean business cards?

Yes. cardlio reads cards in 28 languages on your device, including Japanese, Chinese and Korean. Chinese and Japanese cards are also translated on the device, and the translation is kept with the card and searchable. A Japanese front and an English back become one contact.

Can I tag cards with my own sectors, like chartering or ship management?

Yes. Add your own industries under Settings → Industries & Interests, for example Chartering, Ship management or Port agency. Tagging, search and the Stats chart then use them, and the list syncs to your other devices.

Does it work in an exhibition hall without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Scanning, recognition, rating and tagging all work offline. The Apple Maps address check and iCloud sync catch up once you are back online.
